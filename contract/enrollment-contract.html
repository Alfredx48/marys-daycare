Rate/Payment Arrangements

Rate:

Fees

  1. Weekly rate of $ to be paid on Friday for the following week. Payment is required if the child is absent due to illness, vacation, etc. The daycare is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 am – 5:30 pm (9.5 hours).
  2. There is an overtime fee of $5 for every fifteen minutes after the scheduled pickup time.
  3. Part-time rate of $ per day per child, to be paid in advance.
  4. A two (2) week deposit is required and will be used as payment for the last two (2) weeks.

Holidays

  1. The Daycare Provider will be paid the full rate for all holidays. Holidays are as follows: Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Easter (2 days), Memorial Day, Juneteenth, July 4th, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving (2 days), Christmas (2 days), and New Year’s (2 days).
  2. The Daycare Provider is entitled to a two (2) week paid vacation once per year. At least two (2) weeks’ notice will be given.

Illness

  1. The Daycare Provider will take care of a child with a minor illness, such as a cold.
  2. If illness should occur after arriving, the child will be isolated and the parent will be called to pick up the child.
  3. All medication is to be provided by the parent.

Provisions

  1. Food and drink are to be provided by the parents unless the child is on the Daycare Provider’s food program.
  2. Diapers and extra clothes are to be provided by the parent.
  3. If your child is on medication that needs to be administered while in my program, the medicine must be in the original container and labeled with the child’s name and the doctor’s name. I also have a form for you to sign giving permission for the medication.

Potty Training

  1. The Daycare Provider will potty train children. The child must be ready, at the Daycare Provider’s discretion. It is important that both the parents and the Daycare Provider work with the child.

Notice

  1. Parents must call as soon as possible if they are going to be late or if their child is going to be absent that day.
  2. Two (2) weeks’ notice or two (2) weeks’ pay is required when your child leaves the Daycare Provider’s care.
  3. If you find it necessary for someone else to pick up your child, you must provide written consent in advance.

I have read and fully understand this contract. By signing this agreement, I agree to comply with all terms herein.
