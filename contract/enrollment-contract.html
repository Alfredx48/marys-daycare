Rate/ Payment Arrangements

Rate

Fees

  1. Weekly rate of $ dollars to be paid on Friday for the following week. Payment is required if child is absent due to illness, vacation etc. The daycare open Monday thru Friday 8:00 am - 5:30 pm ( 9.5 hours ).
  2. There is an overtime fee of $5 for every fifteen minutes after the scheduled pickup time.
  3. Part-time rate of $ per day per child to be paid in advance.
  4. A two (2) weeks deposit is required and will be used as payment for the last two (2) weeks.

Holidays

  1. The daycare provider will be paid in full for all Holidays at full rates. Holidays are as follows: Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Easter (2 days), Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veteran’s Day, Thanksgiving (2 days) and Christmas (2 days), New year’s (2 days).
  2. Daycare Provider is entitled to five (5) paid vacation days per year. There will be at least two (2) weeks notice.

Illness

  1. Daycare Provider will take care of child for minor illness such as a colds.
  2. If Illness should occur after arriving, the child will be isolated and the parent will be called to pick up the child.
  3. All medication is to be provided by the parent.

Provisions

  1. Food and drink is to be provided by the parents unless the child is on Daycare Provider’s food program
  2. Diapers and Extra clothes are to be provided by the parent
  3. If your child is on medication and needs to be administered while he is in my program, the medicine must be in the original container and labeled with the child’s name, doctor’s name, I also have a form for you to sign giving the medication.

Potty Training

  1. The Daycare provider will potty train children. The child must be ready at the Daycare Providers discretion. It is important that both parents and the Day care Provider work with the child.

Notice

  1. Parents must call as soon as possible, if you are going to be late your child or if your child is going to be absent for that day.
  2. Two (2) weeks notice or two (2) weeks pay is required when your child leaves the Daycare Provider’s care.
  3. If you find it necessary for someone else to pick up your child, you must provide a written consent in advance
